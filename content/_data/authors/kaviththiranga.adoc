Kavith Lokuhewage is a maintainer of link:https://openchoreo.dev/[OpenChoreo], an open-source internal developer platform, where he leads development of its Backstage-based portal with a focus on simplifying platform and developer experiences. He is an Associate Director and Architect at WSO2 with nearly 13 years of experience building developer tools and portals — from Eclipse-based tooling, through the move to VS Code, to developer tooling for the Ballerina language (Low Code editor, Data Mapper, IDE extensions, and Language Server) and leading the Choreo SaaS portal team.
