Freyam is a founding engineer at Overmind in London, building infrastructure for observability, optimization, fine-tuning and inference for teams shipping AI agents. Before that, he was the first engineer at Realm Labs, an AI/ML security startup in the San Francisco Bay Area, where he spent nearly three years taking the product from zero to a top 10 finalist at the RSAC 2026 Innovation Sandbox. Along the way he interned with Oracle's cloud security team and did AI security and governance research with the AI Vulnerability Database (AVID), including work published at CHI '24.

Open source is where he learned to build. He has contributed to Dask, Joomla, Google Cirq, LLVM MLIR, OpenMRS and Jenkins through Google Summer of Code and beyond. He came back to Jenkins as a GSoC 2023 mentor for the GitLab Plugin Modernization project. He holds a B.Tech (Hons.) in Computer Science from IIIT Hyderabad.
